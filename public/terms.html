Terms of use

Simple rules for a free, open tool.

Last updated 27 September 2026

Free to use

Chittara is free for learning, teaching, festivals and personal projects. Its code is open source under the MIT licence.

Your designs are yours

Designs you make in the studio, and the pictures and files you download, are yours to use as you like. Traditional kolam, rangoli and alpana patterns belong to everyone; please share them with the same respect.

Photos you upload

Only upload photos you have the right to use, and nothing unlawful or hurtful. How photos are handled is described in Privacy and data safety.

Fair use of the service

Please don't overload the service or try to break it. Each visitor can read a limited number of photos per minute so that it stays available for everyone.

Accuracy

Reading a photo is automatic and can be wrong, especially with blurry, tilted or crowded photos. The results and drawing guides are for learning; check them against the real design.

No guarantee

Chittara is provided as it is, without warranty. It is run on free hosting and may sometimes be slow or unavailable.

Who we are

Chittara is an independent student project. It is not an official Government of India website and is not endorsed by any government body.

Contact

Questions or problems: open an issue on GitHub.
