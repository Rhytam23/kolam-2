Privacy and data safety

What happens to your photos and designs, in plain words.

Last updated 27 September 2026

Chittara is built to need as little of your data as possible. There are no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. The short version:

Photos you choose to readSent to our server, read in memory, and deleted as soon as the answer is sent back. Never stored, never shared, never used to train anything.
Designs you saveKept only on your own device, in your browser. We never receive them.
Accounts, names, email, phoneNot collected. There is nothing to sign up for.
Location, contacts, microphoneNot used. "Take a photo" opens your camera only when you tap it, and only to pick a photo.
Cookies, analytics, advertisingNone. No third-party services are loaded; even the fonts come from our own server.
Data shared or soldNone.
EncryptionYes. Everything travels over HTTPS.
Deleting your dataRemove saved designs with Delete in Saved kolams, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. There is nothing of yours on our server to delete.

Your photos

When you choose Read a design, your phone first makes the photo smaller (at most 1600 pixels across), then sends it to our server. The server finds the dots, lines, symmetry and colours, sends the result back to your browser, and throws the photo away. It is never written to disk and never logged.

Please only use photos you took yourself or have permission to use, and avoid photos that show people's faces or house numbers.

Designs you save and the offline app

Designs you save are kept in your browser's storage on your device. If you install Chittara as an app, the pages and pictures it needs are also saved on your device so the studio, guide and practice work without internet. You can remove all of this by clearing the site's data, or by uninstalling the app.

What the hosting service records

Like every website, the service that hosts Chittara keeps short-lived technical logs (time, the address requested and the visitor's IP address) to keep it running and to stop abuse. These logs never contain your photos. To keep the service fair for everyone, each visitor can read a limited number of photos per minute.

Children

Chittara is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone.

Check it yourself

Chittara is open source, so anyone can read exactly what it does: github.com/Rhytam23/kolam-2.
